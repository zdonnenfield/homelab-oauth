Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-02

  • Who uses it: only the owner of this GitHub account, on their own home server. There are no other users.
  • What it accesses: the owner's own Gmail, read-only (scope gmail.readonly). It never sends, changes or deletes email.
  • What it keeps: PDF attachments from senders the owner chose, saved to the owner's private storage, plus a list of message IDs it has already handled.
  • Sharing: nothing is sold, shared or sent to anyone else. Data accessed through Google APIs is not used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Removing access: the owner can revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
